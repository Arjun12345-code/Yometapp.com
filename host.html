Yomet Host Rules

These rules and requirements apply to all Yomet hosts, whether you are new to livestreaming or already have experience as a live host.

1

Hosts are strictly prohibited from registering or operating multiple accounts.

2

Hosts must not showcase pornographic or explicit content during public live broadcasts. According to the original policy, this restriction does not apply to private video calls.

3

Hosts must not transfer their account to another agent. Agents must also not persuade a host to create an independent Yomet agency.

4

All Yomet hosts must be 18 years old or older to work on the platform.
