Yomet Agency and Host Withdrawal Information

  • Minimum withdrawal is $10 ($1=10.000 Beans), and must be a multiple of $10
  • Unused Beans accumulate for future withdrawal.
  • Settlement runs Monday to Sunday and payments are processed every Monday at 10:00 AM (UTC+7).
  • Sub-agency commission formula: (Your daily ratio - sub-agent daily ratio) × sub-agent daily revenue.

How to Withdraw on Olamet

  1. 1. Open your profile in the Yomet app.
  2. 2. Select My Income.
  3. 3. Click Withdraw.
  4. 4. Open payout settings.
  5. 5. Fill in withdrawal details.
  6. 6. Select payment method.
  7. Then enter the amount and submit once finished
